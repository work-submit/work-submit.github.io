Abstract

Mobile infrared-visible imaging typically pairs a compact infrared sensor with a high-resolution visible camera for complementary perception. While cross-sensor misalignment caused by different optics, viewpoints, fields of view, and exposure timings hinders practical deployment. In this paper, we propose BeyondFusion, a unified latent diffusion framework for calibration-free visible-guided infrared super-resolution and infrared-visible fusion tasks. The proposed framework supports both task-specific training and joint training where two tasks are optimized and executed as two readouts of the same generative process. Instead of relying on explicit registration or geometric warping, BeyondFusion introduces a cross-modal self-aligning (CMSA) module into the denoising U-Net. CMSA reorganizes infrared and visible latent tokens into a shared attention space to learn content-adaptive cross-modal correspondence during the denoising process. Together with misalignment augmentation module, the model is facilitated to exploit visible structural and semantic cues while preserving thermal consistency, enabling high-frequency infrared reconstruction and informative fused-image generation under uncalibrated conditions. Extensive experiments on public benchmarks and a mobile infrared-visible imaging system show strong performance across aligned inputs, low-resolution infrared observations, synthetic misalignments, and real mobile captures with unsynchronized sensors. Ablation studies, unified training analysis, and downstream pedestrian detection further validate the effectiveness of BeyondFusion for calibration-free multimodal imaging.

Framework Overview

BeyondFusion formulates infrared super-resolution and infrared-visible fusion as two outputs of a shared latent generative framework. Visible and infrared inputs are encoded into the latent space of a pretrained one-step diffusion model.

Mobile Infrared-Visible Imaging System

The two sensors capture images without geometric calibration or hardware synchronization, naturally introducing viewpoint, field-of-view, resolution, and timing discrepancies. The infrared module has a native resolution of 64×64, while the smartphone visible camera captures high-resolution 512×512 images.

Infrared Image Super-Resolution

BeyondFusion is evaluated against representative visible-guided, infrared-specific, and diffusion-based super-resolution baselines. On public infrared-visible benchmarks, BeyondFusion achieves the best LPIPS score and second-best PSNR, SSIM, MANIQA, and MUSIQ scores, demonstrating a strong balance between perceptual realism and structural fidelity.

Infrared-Visible Image Fusion

BeyondFusion also supports infrared-visible image fusion within the same latent formulation. We evaluate fusion under three conditions: aligned inputs with controlled infrared resolution gaps, simulated misaligned inputs, and real mobile captures. Compared with CDDFuse, Mask-Difuser, and C-OPDR, BeyondFusion preserves clearer visible structures and thermal saliency, while reducing ghosting and misplaced highlights under low-resolution or misaligned infrared inputs.

Downstream Applications

We also evaluate pedestrian detection on fused images using the pretrained Grounded-SAM model in a zero-shot setting. BeyondFusion improves mAP across IoU thresholds compared with aligned fusion baselines and the unregistered fusion baseline, indicating that fewer fusion artifacts and clearer pedestrian structures benefit downstream perception.
