Overview

Camera array-based imaging uses a large number of inexpensive cameras to create virtual cameras that outperform real ones. They can function in many ways by changing the arrangement and aiming of the cameras. We developed an unstructured gigapixel array camera, UnstructuredCam, beyond the resolution of a single camera and human visual perception, to capture large-scale dynamic scenes with both wide field of view and high resolution. The prototype uses one global camera and 19 local cameras, with all cameras mounted on gimbals for adjustable poses.

Online Calibration and Robust Reconfiguration

By proposing an unstructured embedding algorithm, UnstructuredCam is robust to local camera movement, loss, and addition because it can react and recover quickly through online recalibration. During real-time streaming, a local camera can be adjusted and calibrated online within a short time. In this demonstration, the camera moves from the red position to the yellow position.

Demo 02 / Pose adjustment and recovery

Large-Scale 3D Videography

UnstructuredCam can also be extended for distant large-scale 3D videography, enabling immersive virtual-reality experiences from large outdoor scenes.
